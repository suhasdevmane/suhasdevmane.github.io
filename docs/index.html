OntoSage answers plain-English questions about one building from that building's own data: its Brick model in a graph database, the databases that hold its sensor readings, and its documents and floor plans. Every figure in an answer comes from those stores, and when the data is not there the answer says so. These pages cover running it, connecting a building of your own, and what happens between a question and its answer.
