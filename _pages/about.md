**OntoSage is an agentic conversational layer over one smart building's own data** — connect a
building's Brick/BACnet ontology and its time-series, then ask it anything in plain English. Answers are
grounded in live data (SPARQL over GraphDB + time-series + floor plans + the OCBV vocabulary), framed for
the person asking, and can span several intents in one turn.
