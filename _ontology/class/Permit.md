## Definition

An authorisation to carry out controlled work (hot works, roof access, confined space, electrical isolation) for a bounded period in a bounded place. Open or closed is READ from recordStatus, never inferred from the dates: a permit past its end date that was never closed is an exception worth reporting, not a closed permit.

## Examples

- which permits are open in the basement? -&gt; Permit where recordStatus = open and the location matches.
