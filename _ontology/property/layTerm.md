## Definition

A natural-language phrase an occupant uses to express this concept. Multiple values per concept are allowed. The HBCO analogue of ontosage:layTerms for environment concepts.

## Examples

- `hbco:Concept_Stuffy hbco:layTerm "stuffy", "airless", "needs fresh air" .`
